At My Calculator Pro, available as a mobile application, one of our main priorities is the privacy of our users. This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected and recorded by My Calculator Pro and how it is used. By using the app, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. Information We Collect

My Calculator Pro is designed to work mainly as an offline utility tool (Calculator, Zakat Calculator, Age Calculator, Quotation, Inventory, and similar features).

  • Core functionality data: All calculations, notes and local records that you enter are stored only on your device using a local database (Room DB). We do not collect, store or transmit your personal calculation data to any external server.
  • Automatically collected information (ads): To keep the app free, we use third-party advertising partners, such as Google AdMob. AdMob may automatically collect certain non-personal information such as device identifiers, IP address, ad interaction data and general location data in order to serve ads.

We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, phone number, email address or contacts through the app.

Ads are requested only as permitted by the choice you make.
  • Changing your consent later: Users in these regions can review or change their consent choice at any time from the privacy options available in the app.
  • Learn more: You can learn how Google uses data when you use our partners' sites or apps at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services and in Google's Privacy Policy and Terms.
  • Opting out of personalized ads: On your device you can reset or limit the advertising ID from your device settings (Android: Settings > Privacy > Ads).

Your records and calculations stay on your device. We do not keep copies on any server. You can delete your data at any time by clearing the app's storage in your device settings or by uninstalling the app, which removes all locally stored data.
